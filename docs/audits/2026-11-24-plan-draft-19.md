## What was checked and held

### Internal consistency and fidelity to sources

- Version and history claims in section 12 hold against git: the DspTap spike branched at 0.3.0 and origin/main's version.h says 0.7.0; SampleRateTap's spike is at 0.8.0 and origin/main at 0.9.0 plus three commits; a chain reports window_frames() from DspTap 0.5.0 (commit 4b30a02); the Butterworth stage is on main from 0.6.0; SampleRateTap 0.9.0 carries rational's recursive stages, the guard band filter and their C ABI.
- Section 3.1's trait table matches the source on origin/main: sample_traits (Q1.14 and Q1.30 coefficients, int64 accumulator, one round-half-up point, saturating output, keyed on int16_t/int32_t), iir_traits in iir_kernels.h (Q2.29 and Q2.60 states with two guard bits, Q2.30 coefficients, exact 64- and 96-bit products, floor or magnitude truncation chosen per section against limit cycles, saturation at the output only), fft_arith (Q1.30 twiddles, saturating add/sub, shr_round, headroom_bits).
- FFT facts in 5.2, 3.1 and 7.1 that hold: DspTap packs the Nyquist real part at index 1 and uses exp(+i) with an unnormalized inverse (fft.h lines 356-366); the fixed scaling policy's exponent is a constant of the length (log2 N for Q15, log2 N + 1 for Q31), so a spectrum's format can be a compile-time type; the CMSIS engine serves 32 to 4096 only; vDSP is split complex and allocates when its setup is created; DspTap needs an inline-namespace ABI tag on every class embedding the FFT.
- The biquad_cascade_block figure is right: bench/README.md on origin/main shows 0.26 to 0.52 at orders 2 to 8 across the m4f, m33, m55 and hexagon columns (float, calls of 128 frames, 1 to 12 channels). The latency example in 9.2, {4.02, 2.51} frames for order 4 at fc = 0.1, matches butterworth.h's stated numbers, as do the card's method citation and even-order restriction.
- The spike's layout numbers are quoted correctly from bench/README.md on branch ccr-ef01ff18-nxz8n4: the FFT FIR is cheaper interleaved by 4 to 10 % on the FPU M-class cores and 14 to 16 % on Hexagon (within 1 % on the soft-float M4); planar is indifferent to a run-time channel count within 2 %; runs 153, 157 and 161 are the ones cited there. SampleRateTap's tools/layout/README.md on its spike branch supports 'the converter rows stand' (interleaved never the more expensive layout). fft_fir.h and bench/layout exist only on the spike branch; the plugin's page-boundary fix is commit 4ae096f.
- SampleRateTap fidelity: spsc_ring.h is under async/include/tap/sr/async/ and is a wait-free bulk SPSC ring; async's converter is push/pull on two threads with a status() snapshot of state (filling/locked), ppm estimate, fill, underruns and overruns, which fits section 6's junction table; rational's basic_stage (stage.h) has exact outputs_for and frames_needed on a compile-time L/M schedule, as D18 assumes; ceilings.py, icount.py and the callmax worst-single-call scenarios exist as section 8 says.
- Decision statuses against the plan's own git history (/home/user/catenae, read-only): the 'I mostly agree' review is draft 7 and no row marked accepted was introduced after it without its own note; D33 matches the relayed instruction to defer the license; both source repositories are MIT with a single human author. No leftover result<>, to<>, public k_ constants or process/run confusion was found in sections 1 to 9; the k_ names in the sketches are caller-side constants.
- Arithmetic in the sketches and text checks out: delay<2, 480> of float is 3,840 bytes and 10 ms at 48 kHz; fir<63, 2> needs 62 * 2 history samples; Q4.27 gives 4 * 6.02 = 24 dB of headroom; stft<512, 128> yields 257 bins and the region's ratio composes to 1:1. One sketch detail does not compile as written: process(span<const S, N * Ch>, span<S, M * Ch>) cannot deduce N and M (g++ 13.3: 'couldn't deduce template parameter N'), so the fixed-extent overload must take the extents themselves and static_assert divisibility by Ch.

Scaffold: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/dd231a64-4f4f-561b-96b1-85c53a22809c/scratchpad/audit/cpp17/catenae.h, s94.h, s95.h.
- `static constexpr ratio rate_ratio{1, 1};` as a class-type static member works in C++17. It is read through stage_traits with no probing, composed across a chain by a fold, and compared in a message-less static_assert: `stage_traits<decltype(overdrive)>::rate_ratio == ratio{1, 1}` holds for 9.3, and for 9.4 (1/128 then 128/1, reduced with constexpr std::gcd).
- The brace-initialised temporaries of 9.3 and 9.4 parse and need no class template argument deduction. `map<2, soft_clip>{{4.0f}}` works with an explicit constexpr constructor, and the result type is the flattened `chain<up2<2>, up2<2>, map<2, soft_clip>, down2<2>, down2<2>>`.
- A chain can size its scratch at compile time from each stage's static constexpr max_outputs_for across rate changes and across port kinds (bytes, int32, float, complex<float>), as a std::tuple of std::arrays built with if constexpr recursion. The 9.3 chain runs correctly (384 frames over three calls) and a mismatched joint yields exactly one error.
- A namespace-scope chain built with `|` is constant-initialised when every stage constructor and operator| are constexpr: no start-up code and no start-up stack, on arm-none-eabi-g++ 13.2 for Cortex-M4 at -O0, -O2 and -Os, and on hosted g++-14 and clang-18.
- `[[nodiscard]]` on the expected class template and on `enum class status` works in C++17. Discarding `butterworth_design<4>::make(0.2)` or a function returning status is an error under -Werror on g++-14 and clang-18. `if (!designed) return designed.error();` and `butterworth<4, 2> lowpass(*designed)` work with an explicit operator bool and a union-based, constexpr expected for trivially destructible designs.
- `std::transform` writing over its own input through catenae::span iterators is correct at run time under AddressSanitizer and UBSan (gain in place: 1..8 becomes 0.5..4). `std::tanh(float)` in soft_clip raises no -Wdouble-promotion.

It also does not fire for a local named after a member function (latency, tail_frames). 7.2's statement that `static constexpr ratio ratio{1, 1}` is impossible is right for GCC (`changes meaning of 'ratio'`); Clang accepts it.

### No heap, ROM, real time, the compiled core

- Wait-free junction indices are portable to the Arm targets in C++17: with arm-none-eabi-g++ 13.2.1, std::atomic<size_t>, <uint32_t> and <float> are is_always_lock_free on cortex-m4, m33 and m55 and link without libatomic. The carried spsc_ring uses only load and store on size_t. 64-bit atomics (uint64_t, double) are not lock-free there and fail to link (`__atomic_fetch_add_8`), but SampleRateTap's async telemetry already keeps to 32-bit atomics, so nothing carried is unportable.

### 63. A single-span planar port cannot be sub-blocked, which conflicts with the chain's block-at-a-time process() and nested run()

- **Lens:** architecture
- **Where:** section 3.1 (planar port), 3.2 (run takes spans of the ports' value types), section 4, 5.1, section 13 question 1
- **Severity:** minor as reported, minor after verification
- **Verdict:** confirmed

**Problem.** Question 1 offers "one span with the channels end to end" as an option, and 3.2 already writes run(in, out) as one span each. The chain's process() accepts any length and works through it in 64-frame blocks, and an enclosing chain calls an inner chain's run() with more than one block. A 64-frame slice of an N-frame end-to-end planar buffer is not contiguous: channel c's slice starts at c*N + offset. So a chain whose outer port is planar (one ending in `deinterleave`, or a nested chain of mono stages) cannot hand its first or last stage a block without a copy, or it silently produces "planar per 64-frame block", which breaks "bit-identical for any chunking" as the caller sees it. "Contiguous within a call" does not say whether the call is process() or run().

**Recommendation.** Record the constraint with question 1. Either choose a span (or pointer) per channel, which sub-blocks trivially and changes run()'s signature for planar ports in 3.2, or keep one span and add a compile-time rule that a planar port may not be a chain's outer port and that a planar run inside a chain lives in one scratch block. Add that rule to C0's compile-fail battery.

**Second reviewer.** By geometry (no compile needed): in an N-frame end-to-end planar buffer channel c starts at c*N, so a 64-frame sub-block is not contiguous, while section 4's process() works a block at a time and 3.2 writes run(in, out) with one span each. Nothing in the plan forbids a planar port at a chain's outer edge (deinterleave is an ordinary stage and a chain is a stage). It is a constraint to record with open question 1, which is already open.

No compile test is needed for the geometry; confidence is medium only because the plan may intend planar never to appear at a chain's outer port, which it does not say.
